## 摘要
Abstract—Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures have be-
come essential for scaling large language models, driving the
development of specialized device-initiated communication li-
braries such as DeepEP, Hybrid-EP, and others. These libraries
demonstrate the performance benefits of GPU-initiated RDMA
for MoE dispatch and combine operations.
This paper presents NCCL EP (Expert Parallelism), a ground-
up MoE communication library built entirely on NCCL’s De-
vice API. NCCL EP provides unified ncclEpDispatch and
ncclEpCombine primitives with both C and Python interfaces,
supporting Low-Latency (LL) mode for inference decoding and
High-Throughput (HT) mode for training and inference prefill.
LL targets small batch sizes (1–128 tokens) using direct all-to-all
RDMA+NVLink mesh connectivity with double-buffered commu-
nication for overlapping dispatch and combine phases. HT targets
large batches (4096+ tokens) using hierarchical communication
that aggregates tokens within NVLink domains before inter-node
RDMA transmission. Both modes leverage Device API for both
intra- and inter-node communications, taking advantage of its
topology awareness and optimized GPU-initiated implementation.
We evaluate NCCL EP on an H100-based cluster across multi-
node configurations, demonstrating competitive LL kernel perfor-
mance and presenting end-to-end results with vLLM integration.

## 关键句
- MoE Communication Patterns The MoE models extend the Transformer architecture, im- plementing the FFN as a network of experts, distributed among available GPUs [1], [2].
- During forward propagation, token embeddings are routed to a subset of assigned experts, and expert responses are gathered back.
- On the backward pass, the loss gradients are routed to the experts, who provided answers, to update their parameters.
- Dispatch routes tokens from source GPUs to the GPUs hosting their assigned experts.
- A gating network selects top- k experts per token, producing routing indices that define a dynamic, irregular communication pattern.
- Combine aggregates the expert outputs back to the original token locations.
- When tokens visit multiple experts (top-k > 1), combine performs a weighted summation of expert outputs according to gating weights.
- • Load imbalance: Popular “hot” experts receive more tokens, creating asymmetric communication volumes.
- Dispatch (ncclEpDispatch) sends tokens to their assigned experts.
- NCCL EP execution flow: Group creation (once), followed by repeated Handle→Dispatch→Expert FFN→Combine cycles.
- Combine (ncclEpCombine) gathers expert outputs and re- duces them back to the original token order.
- The group config- uration is determined by the algorithm mode (LL or HT), number of experts, maximum tokens per rank, and buffer- sizing parameters.
